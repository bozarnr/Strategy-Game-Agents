# Disclosure Boundary

This repository publishes clean-room game-agent tooling and bounded evidence records, not competition secrets.

## Included

- Synthetic repeated-game engines, baseline agents, trace validation, fitting, and tests.
- A generic evidence ladder for candidate readiness.
- Tiny sample records that show how candidate evidence can be tracked.

## Excluded

- Complete competition strategies, private opponent logs, exact challenge prompts, hidden parameters, credentials, and submission automation.
- Any claim that a local simulation result solves a live competition or trading game.

## Sample Data Rule

Files under `sample_data/` are synthetic examples for schema and reporting only.
